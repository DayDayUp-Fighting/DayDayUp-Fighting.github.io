# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **GameJoy Mahjong** (the "App", published as `com.start.gamejoymahjong` on Google Play) handles information. We built this App to be playable offline and to collect as little personal data as possible. Please read it carefully.

**Contact:** mumujianghuxing@gmail.com

## 1. Information the App Collects

**Information you provide:** We do not require an account, sign-up, or any personal information to play. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment information.

**Information collected automatically (via the App and its third-party advertising partners):**

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Device identifiers | Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID); an app-scoped random identifier generated locally and stored on your device | Advertising (serving, frequency capping, measurement); analytics |
| App activity | Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, ad completion), gameplay progress stored on your device | Advertising; app functionality |
| App info and performance | Device model, brand, OS version, app version, memory/disk availability | Analytics and app functionality |
| Approximate location (non-precise) | Coarse location derived from IP address or network region | Advertising (regional ad compliance and relevance) |

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, photos, files, health data, financial data, or biometric data. The App requests no such permissions.

## 2. Third-Party Advertising Partners

The App shows ads through the following SDKs, which process data as independent controllers under their own privacy policies:

- **AppLovin (MAX mediation)** — Privacy Policy: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/
- **Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK** — Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** — used to obtain and store your consent choices (EEA/UK)

These partners may use device identifiers and app activity to deliver and measure ads. Where required by law (EEA/UK), we obtain your consent before serving personalized ads, via a Google-certified consent management platform (UMP). You can change your choice at any time by clearing the App's data or (where available) through the App's privacy settings entry.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

- Gameplay progress and the app-scoped identifier are stored **locally on your device** (app-private storage). Uninstalling the App deletes them.
- We do not operate our own servers that store your personal data.
- Data transmitted to advertising partners is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## 4. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of data associated with your use of the App by contacting us at **mumujianghuxing@gmail.com**. Since we do not maintain user accounts, uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data; advertising partners' data is handled per their own privacy policies (AppLovin and Google each provide deletion/ads-settings mechanisms, e.g. Google Ads Settings and AppLovin's privacy request channels).

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

## 6. Your Rights (EEA/UK — GDPR; California — CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to processing or withdraw consent. Since the App itself does not maintain user databases, requests relating to advertising-partner data are exercised through the partners' channels above; you may also contact us at **mumujianghuxing@gmail.com** and we will assist and direct your request.

California residents: we do not "sell" or "share" personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA for cross-context behavioral advertising without required notices and opt-outs handled through the consent flows described in Section 2.

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. The "Last updated" date above shows the current version. Material changes will be reflected in the App's listing and, where practicable, in-App notice.
